# Current market check

No stored price is current. Check prices at the time of the recommendation and label the checking date. Include market region and currency when the user voluntarily provides them.

If exact SKU, regional variant, or market region is unavailable:

- continue with a category-level price range;
- state which assumptions were used;
- mark compatibility and pricing confidence as lower;
- never pressure the user to reveal location, serial number, or purchase account details.

## Sources

Use at least one and preferably two current sources:

- manufacturer or official store price;
- major retailer listing;
- reputable price-tracking page;
- recent used-market comparables.

Do not use rental listings, deposits, sponsored placeholders, unrelated bundles, or implausibly low bait prices as the market price.

## Price types

When the exact SKU is known, record separately:

- official MSRP or launch price;
- current ordinary retail price;
- active promotion or subsidy;
- used asking price;
- realistic used sale value.

## Net cost

Record what will happen to the old part before calculating cost:

- retained: no cash offset;
- sold: subtract only realistic sale proceeds after fees;
- gifted: no cash offset;
- undecided: show cash cost without a resale offset and label the remaining choice.

Calculate:

> cash upgrade cost = delivered new-part cost - confirmed sale proceeds (only if the old part will actually be sold)

> economic comparison value = cash upgrade cost, with the value of any retained old part reported separately as an asset rather than deducted from cash outlay

Example: a CNY 5,000 new card with a CNY 1,500 old card has a CNY 5,000 cash cost if the old card is retained, but CNY 3,500 before fees if it is actually sold.

Include:

- shipping;
- tax;
- platform or resale fees;
- adapter, cable, cooler, PSU, or case costs;
- warranty differences.

## Freshness

- Flash promotion: same-day evidence only.
- Ordinary retail listing: refresh at least weekly.
- Used price: use recent comparable listings and note condition variance.
- If no reliable current data is available, state this clearly and do not provide a price verdict.

## Output

State:

- checking date;
- region and currency if provided, otherwise say that market region is unspecified;
- price range, not a false single exact value;
- whether the current part will be kept, sold, or gifted;
- the net cash cost;
- what price would change the recommendation.
